A pastry chef's own bakery

Kelsey ran the pastry kitchen for Daniel Boulud before she opened her own. The short version:

  1. Charlotte

    Johnson & Wales University: degrees in baking and pastry arts, culinary arts and food service management.

  2. Yssingeaux, France

    Pastry at ENSP, Alain Ducasse's school, with kitchen time in Rome, Barcelona and Paris.

  3. 2016, Washington

    Executive pastry chef at Daniel Boulud's DBGB at 23. She ran the pastry kitchen there for three years.

  4. 2018

    Nominated for a James Beard Rising Star award.

  5. 2020

    Won Food Network's Chopped Sweets, in the episode “Million Dollar Desserts”, and started baking for Augusta from home.

  6. 2021, Evans

    Bottom Line Bakery & Café opens on Washington Road.

  7. 2025

    Voted Best Bakery in Augusta Magazine's Best of Augusta.
